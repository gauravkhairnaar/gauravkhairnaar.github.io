How I got here

I started with a Diploma in Computer Engineering, moved into a B.Tech in Computer Science, and most recently completed CDAC ACTS's PGCP-AC program, rounding out my backend and AI-integration skills before stepping into industry. Along the way I've built a voice-triggered safety app, a reactive AI microservice for a farming platform, and a full-stack chat product on my own.

I like problems with a clear system underneath them — services that talk to each other, data that needs a sane shape, APIs that fail loudly instead of quietly. Outside of coursework, most of my time goes into DSA practice and closing gaps by building real, deployable things rather than toy demos.
